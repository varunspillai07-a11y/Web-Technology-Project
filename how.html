From Planning to Performance

Understand how PBL transforms logistics into a results-driven system.

Step 1: Define Performance Metrics

The first step is to clearly define what success looks like. Metrics such as availability, reliability, and response time are identified to measure performance.

⬇️

Step 2: Set Performance Agreements

Contracts are created based on expected outcomes rather than products. Both parties agree on measurable goals and responsibilities.

⬇️

Step 3: Monitor Performance

Continuous tracking ensures that the system is meeting defined goals. Data is collected and analyzed regularly.

⬇️

Step 4: Continuous Improvement

Based on performance data, improvements are made to enhance efficiency, reduce costs, and increase reliability over time.

📊 Simple Flow

Define → Agree → Monitor → Improve
